Founder & Lead Filmmaker

Arjun Mehra

Master Storyteller · Since 2012

"Filmmaking is not just about light and sound — it's about the emotions we preserve and the stories we leave behind for every family."

Arjun designs for couples who celebrate unapologetically. His work — an alchemy of shadow and sentiment — is rooted in narrative, editorial composition, and a deep connection to place.

Born and raised in a small town in Rajasthan, Arjun draws inspiration from the layered beauty of his surroundings: the dramatic glamour of old-world palaces, the rich storytelling traditions of his Indian heritage — influences that shape the emotional depth of his films without dictating a specific aesthetic.

As the founder of theunfilteredstories_, he has built a multi-faceted practice spanning destination weddings, editorial features, and brand films. His portfolio includes collaborations with luxury venues like Taj Lake Palace, Rambagh Palace, and Oberoi Udaivilas, where he serves as a preferred creative partner for discerning families across India and abroad.

His work has been recognised globally, including being named to the prestigious Wedding Filmmakers Guild Top 100 and featured in publications such as Vogue Weddings, Brides Today, and Harper's Bazaar.
